**Sizzle & Serve** is a fast-paced cooking game: orders roll in on the ticket board, and you chop, grill, bake and simmer each dish exactly as the ticket asks, then serve it before the customer's patience runs out. Three shifts — Lunch, Dinner and Midnight Rush — bring faster orders and new recipes (Sizzle Burger, Garden Salad, Pepperoni Pizza, Harvest Soup). Serve fast for tips, chain perfect serves into a combo multiplier, and don't let three orders expire or the kitchen closes.

Everything is drawn in code on one canvas — no images, no libraries, no network requests — with synthesized WebAudio bleeps for chopping, sizzling, coins and alarms.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and play with mouse or keyboard: click ingredient bins (or keys 1–9), click a station to collect when it says READY, Enter serves, P pauses, M mutes, H shows help, R restarts.
